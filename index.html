01 / RESEARCH CONTEXT

Real-world capture.
Multiple perspectives.

RealDynLFV is a large-parallax benchmark for real-world dynamic light field video, comprising 45 dynamic scene sequences recorded from a 5 × 5 camera array.

Multiple viewpoints and scene motion provide a shared setting for studying light field video processing.

Capture & temporal alignment

Camera recordings are temporally aligned in post-processing using audio signals, rather than hardware synchronization.
